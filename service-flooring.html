Engineered Subfloor Preparation for Perfectly Flat Floors

Large-format tile demands an exceptionally flat subfloor. We grind high spots, fill low areas with self-leveling underlayment, and install uncoupling membranes to isolate substrate movement, preventing hollow spots and future tile cracking.

Premium Tile
Installation.

We specialize in the precise installation of high-density porcelain and natural stone. Our team ensures every slab is perfectly aligned and polished to reveal its unique artisanal beauty. Tile installations in Florida require special attention to humidity, substrate deflection, and temperature shifts. We implement uncoupling membranes (Schluter-DITRA) and modified mortars guaranteeing an unyielding bond and zero cracked tiles over time.

  • Zero-lippage laser leveling clips
  • Subfloor grinding & self-leveling
  • Diamond-wheel wet cutting
  • Ultra-narrow grout joints
